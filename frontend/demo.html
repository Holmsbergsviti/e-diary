Chartwell E-Diary

A full-stack school management platform with separate experiences for students, teachers and administrators. Pick a role below to explore it. No sign-up required.

Interactive demo · sample school with 4 classes, 9 teachers and 24 students
🎒

Student

Alex Morgan, class 10A
  • Dashboard with homework, completion progress and recent grades
  • Grades per subject and term, with teacher comments
  • Weekly timetable with events, holidays, substitutes and missed lessons
  • Behaviour notes from teachers
  • Profile with emoji avatars and colour themes
🍎

Teacher

Emma Clarke, Mathematics, form tutor of 10A
  • Take attendance per lesson with late minutes, comments and topics
  • Marks book with weighted averages, categories and class overview
  • Homework with completion tracking and behaviour notes
  • Class statistics, end-of-term reports and Excel/CSV exports
  • Study hall, substitute lessons and class events
🛡️

Admin

Rachel Adams, super admin
  • School overview, full student profiles and attendance conflict flags
  • Manage classes, subjects, teachers, students and admin permissions
  • Edit the timetable, or regenerate it with the timetable generator
  • Subject enrollments, events and holidays
  • "Login as" any student or teacher to see what they see

The demo runs the real frontend against a mock backend in your browser, so it loads instantly and any changes you make stay on your device.
Production stack: vanilla HTML/CSS/JS on Netlify · Django REST API on Render · Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage)
