The school day, in one place.

Chartwell E-Diary is a full-stack school platform for grades, attendance, homework, timetables and reports, with separate experiences for students, teachers and administrators.

Explore as

No sign-up. Runs on sample data in your browser, and you can switch roles at any time.

Three roles, one platform

Each role sees its own dashboard and permissions, backed by the same data.

Student

Alex Morgan · Class 10A
  • Dashboard with homework, completion progress and recent grades
  • Grades per subject and term, with teacher comments
  • Weekly timetable with events, holidays, substitutes and missed lessons
  • Behaviour notes, profile avatars and colour themes

Teacher

Emma Clarke · Mathematics, form tutor 10A
  • Per-lesson attendance with late minutes, comments and topics
  • Marks book with weighted averages and a form-class overview
  • Homework tracking, behaviour notes, study hall and substitutes
  • Class statistics, term reports and Excel/CSV exports

Admin

Rachel Adams · Super admin
  • School overview, student profiles and attendance conflict flags
  • Manage classes, subjects, staff, students and admin permissions
  • Edit the timetable or regenerate it with the built-in generator
  • Log in as any student or teacher to see exactly what they see
